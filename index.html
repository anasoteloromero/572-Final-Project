Wisconsin Deep Time

A visual journey through 4.5 billion years of geologic history.

This interactive timeline explores the bedrock geology of Wisconsin, beginning with the formation of Earth and moving forward through the Archean, Proterozoic, and Paleozoic eras. As you scroll, time moves proportionally to capture the vast scale of deep time and the processes that shaped the landscape beneath your feet.

You can scroll through to also see the what was going on to the whole earth or click through the black dots on the left hand scale to view the major bedrock and geology of Wisconsin.
